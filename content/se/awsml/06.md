## 6. Checkpoint: AI Practitioner

This is the gate to the associate level: take AIF-C01 now. Book it once you score 750 or higher on the official practice question set.

### What the exam looks like

- 65 questions (50 scored and 15 unscored that you cannot tell apart) in 90 minutes.
- Question types: multiple choice, multiple response, ordering and matching. In ordering and matching you must get every item right to score.
- Scaled score from 100 to 1,000; you need 700 to pass. You do not need to pass each domain separately.
- Weights: fundamentals of AI and ML 20%, fundamentals of GenAI 24%, applications of foundation models 28%, responsible AI 14%, security and governance 14%.

The exam does **not** expect you to code models, tune hyperparameters or build pipelines. It tests whether you can choose the right approach and service, and use them responsibly.

### Keywords that decide the answer

| The question says | Usually points to |
|---|---|
| "Least operational overhead" | A managed AI service or Bedrock |
| "Data changes often", "cite sources" | RAG / Bedrock Knowledge Bases |
| "Consistent style or format" | Fine-tuning or few-shot prompting |
| "More deterministic" | Lower temperature |
| "Harmful content", "PII in answers" | Bedrock Guardrails |
| "Explain predictions", "bias" | SageMaker Clarify |
| "Document intended use" | SageMaker Model Cards |
| "Human review of low confidence" | Amazon A2I |

### Mixed practice

1. Which setting makes an FM's output more deterministic? (a) higher temperature (b) lower temperature (c) larger max tokens (d) higher top-k
2. A chatbot must answer from policy PDFs that change weekly. Lowest effort? (a) fine-tune monthly (b) RAG with a knowledge base (c) continued pre-training (d) a bigger model
3. Which service pulls text and tables from scanned forms? (a) Comprehend (b) Rekognition (c) Textract (d) Kendra
4. A model scores 99% on training data and 70% on validation. What is wrong? (a) underfitting (b) overfitting (c) high bias (d) nothing
5. Which metric compares a generated summary with a reference? (a) RMSE (b) ROUGE (c) AUC (d) silhouette score
6. Who is responsible for encrypting your training data in S3? (a) AWS (b) the customer (c) the model provider (d) nobody
7. Which document describes a model's intended use, data and limits? (a) SageMaker Model Card (b) CloudTrail log (c) IAM policy (d) service quota
8. Which Bedrock feature blocks harmful content and redacts PII? (a) Agents (b) Guardrails (c) Prompt Management (d) model evaluation
9. What counts as a hallucination? (a) slow output (b) confident output that is factually wrong (c) a refusal (d) a token limit cut-off
10. Which tool detects bias and explains SageMaker model predictions? (a) SageMaker Clarify (b) Macie (c) Inspector (d) Config
11. Put the RAG steps in order: retrieve similar chunks; chunk and embed documents; generate an answer with the chunks in the prompt; embed the question.
12. Which service provides AWS compliance reports such as SOC 2? (a) Audit Manager (b) Artifact (c) Config (d) Trusted Advisor

**Answers.** 1b, 2b, 3c, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7a, 8b, 9b, 10a. 11: chunk and embed documents → embed the question → retrieve similar chunks → generate. 12b.

### Booking and exam day

1. Sign in at aws.training/certification with your AWS Builder ID and schedule through Pearson VUE.
2. Online proctoring needs a quiet room, a clear desk, a webcam and the system test run the day before.
3. Flag any question that takes more than 90 seconds, and come back at the end. Answer everything.
4. After you pass, look in your AWS Certification account for a discount voucher for your next exam.
