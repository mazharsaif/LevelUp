## 12. Measure & protect

This skill covers the free "after launch" products: crash reports, analytics, remote flags, abuse protection and the local emulator.

### Crashlytics and Analytics

- **Crashlytics** groups crash reports by cause, so you see "this exception on these phones" instead of a pile of logs.
- **Analytics** records events and audiences.
- Both are free on both plans. [checked 2026-11-23, fast-moving]
- Analytics data can be exported to **BigQuery**, which is the natural bridge for a data scientist: raw events, then SQL and models. [consensus]

### Remote Config and A/B testing

**Remote Config** lets you change app behaviour or flip a feature without shipping a release. **A/B Testing** compares variants and reads the result from Analytics. Keep flags few and temporary, and delete them when the experiment ends.

### App Check

App Check attests that a request comes from your genuine app. It is the main defence against scripts abusing your database, your functions or SMS sign-in. Turn on enforcement gradually: watch the metrics first so you do not lock out real users.

### The Emulator Suite and the CLI

`firebase-tools` runs local emulators for Auth, Firestore, Functions and more. You can test rules and code without touching production or spending money. Make "run the rules tests in the emulator" a step in every deploy. [consensus]

### What to add first

| You have | Add first |
|---|---|
| A published app that crashes sometimes | Crashlytics |
| Questions about how people use it | Analytics, then the BigQuery export |
| A risky feature to roll out slowly | Remote Config |
| A public database or SMS sign-in | App Check |
