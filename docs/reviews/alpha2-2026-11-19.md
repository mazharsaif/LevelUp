## Progress, XP and plan
- **Numbers disagree:** A1 shows 0.5% / 0.9% / 1.2% in different places, and grammar shows 0/20 vs 1/33.
- **"x% of A1 to level N" counts down**, which reads like going backwards.
- **Inflated:** after one session, Level 13 and a "projected finish" 9 days away; the 25 XP goal is met in minutes. A grammar chapter counts as fully done after reading plus 3–7 questions.
- **Too technical:** SM-2, "<1d / 2d / 6d" buttons, XP maths on the setup screen.
- **No revision or mock weeks:** `weekPlan` spreads new lessons up to the target date. content.json `goals/roadmap` has a 12-week plan with mocks in weeks 10–12 that the app never shows.
- **Reviews:**
  - self-graded (easy to cheat by always tapping Easy) and 65% English→German
  - the teacher suggests 0 XP for "Again" instead of −0.5, so learners stay honest
  - suggest quick multiple choice, typing or listening checks instead of self-grading
- **Setup:** accepts a 6-day target with no warning. The Auto goal's text is cut off and it doesn't show its XP.

## Exercises and teaching (teacher, exam)
- **Teaching order follows the Goethe topics, not frequency:** *ich* comes in lesson 12, *haben/kommen* 39, *sein* 40, *und/mit* 60, while the grammar that needs them comes in week 2, and *Milliarde* and ordinals come on days 0–3. Add a "core 120" block (pronouns, top verbs, und/aber/nicht, question words) in weeks 1–2. Finish Ch 1 in weeks 1–2.
- **Listening is only single words** (sound → spelling). Add audio → English, dictation and stories.
- **Phrase "What does this mean?" is audio only** and never shows the German, even after a miss.
- **Fill-the-gap and sentence-building:**
  - Weak wrong options: "ein ___ zum Frühstück" offers Brot, which also fits, and the English line gives the answer away.
  - The same sentence is used back to back.
  - Fix: take wrong options from the same topic and gender, and don't reuse a sentence within one part.
- **Never tested:** plurals; verbs that change vowels. Grammar and phrases never enter reviews.
- **Repetition:** phrase parts are 21–23 steps for 4–5 phrases ("Hi!" 4 times in one part).
- **Small things:**
  - a wrong multiple-choice answer should say what the chosen word means
  - matching shows "NOT QUITE" on an empty board
  - the review summary says "Best combo 0"
- **Exam gaps:**
  - Reading: nothing longer than one sentence (emails, ads, signs).
  - Writing: no form-filling and no 30-word message.
  - Speaking: no Teil 2 topic cards and no guided introduction.

## Story listening + answering: combined proposal
**Content model** (`content/stories/sNN.json`, built into `stories[]`):
`{id, title, titleEn, level, after: <path id>, scene: emoji, rate, slowRate, speakers: {K: {name, voice}}, pre: [new words], lines: [{sp, de, en, a, aSlow, words, new, pause}], q: [{t: mc|tf|who|order|gap|pic|num, q, opts, a, line}], shadow: [line idx]}`.
- **Audio:** audio id = sha1(voice|rate|text). `fetch_audio.py` needs a voice and speed per clip. Make separate slow clips; never slow audio in the browser.
- **Word check:** `tools/check_story.py` flags any word not taught before the story's place in the path. Allow at most 2–3 explained new words.
- **Cast:** Ravi Sharma from Indore, moving to Frankfurt (male voice), Frau Klein his teacher and later landlady (Katja), Lena a colleague, Herr Müller (waiter, doctor and so on). Ravi and Frau Klein already appear in p9. The teen tester's idea: a running series with cliffhangers (e.g. "Lena & Bello").

**Levels**
| Stage | Weeks | Length | Speed | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Early | 1–3 | 4–12 lines, 20–45 s | −15% | in English, picture/emoji choice |
| Mid | 4–6 | 12–16 lines | −8% | simple German, tap for English |
| Late | 7–9 | exam style | normal | German only, two modes: Lernen / Prüfung |

**Formats copied from the Goethe exam:**
- H1: dialogue, multiple choice, played twice
- H2: announcement, richtig/falsch, played once
- H3: voicemail, multiple choice, played twice
- Reading: L1 email, L2 ads, L3 signs

Numbers, times, prices and spelling should appear in almost every item, with *vierzehn/vierzig* and digit-swap traps.

**Flow** (`story.js`, 6–8 minutes):
1. Before listening: scene and 0–3 new words with audio. Exam mode: read the questions first.
2. Listen with no text: 2 free plays (one in exam mode for H2), plus a slow toggle.
3. Questions: gist, then detail. After a miss, "hear that part" replays the line.
4. Transcript: line highlighting, tap a line to replay, tap a word for its meaning, English per line, optional Hindi.
5. Shadowing: 3–4 lines, reusing the existing 🎤 step.
6. Retell (mid/late): put events in order or build a summary sentence.

**Question types:**
- multiple choice
- richtig/falsch
- who said it
- put events in order
- fill the gap from audio
- picture (emoji) choice
- numbers
- no typing at early A1

**Placement:**
- A new path item type `story`, about one per week (end of the path week, or after a phrase topic from week 2).
- Counts toward A1 % like a phrase topic, or as a new "Exam skills" share (about 15%).
- A weekly "Prüfungstraining" set of 6 items, and timed mocks in weeks 10–12 with a predicted score per skill.

**Spaced re-listening:** a missed story comes back after 3 → 10 → 30 days (exam items 1/3/7) on Today, missed questions first. Its key words go into reviews. Saved progress: `S.stories[id] = {best, plays, slow, ladder, due, wrongQ}`.

**XP (current small scheme):** +3 for passing listen + questions (+1 if it fails), +1 if perfect, +1 for shadowing, +5 when the story is done (about 10). A re-listen gives +1, and +1 more at 80%+. No negative XP; replays are free.

**TTS:**
- one clip per line, played in sequence
- pauses: 450 ms same speaker, 750 ms speaker change, 1200 ms scene change
- prices written out in words for the voice, digits in the transcript
- letters spelled with dashes so the voice pauses
