LevelUp home-screen widgets

Design mockups for Android widgets (Jetpack Glance, later). Every widget sits on a phone home screen at real launcher cell sizes: 1 cell ≈ 95 × 118 dp on a 412 dp-wide phone, widget = n·95 − 16 by m·118 − 16 dp (2×1 = 174×102, 2×2 = 174×220, 4×1 = 364×102, 4×2 = 364×220, 4×4 = 364×456). Data is Taha's-style German A1 progress: Level 23, D-Rank (Explorer), 22.4 % of A1, 🔥 12-day streak, 48 / 120 XP today, 1 / 3 parts, 14 words due. Data fields, tap targets and Glance notes are in README.md.

A · HunterMatches theme-sl: navy panel, blue glow outline and corner brackets, condensed caps, purple aura on the emblem. Always dark. The glow outline is one static background drawable.
B · CalmMatches theme-calm: warm paper / slate, deep teal, serif headings, flat. Light and dark.
C · Material YouDynamic colour (GlanceTheme): tonal surfaces follow the wallpaper. Shown with a violet seed (night wallpaper) and a teal seed (day wallpaper). Article colours stay fixed (der blue, die red, das green).
