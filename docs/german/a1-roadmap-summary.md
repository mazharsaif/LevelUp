# German A1 roadmap: where it stands (2026-11-24)

Live at https://levelrise.org since version 2026.11.24.05. Detail and reasoning: `a1-situation-roadmap.md`. Duolingo research: `docs/reviews/duolingo-*` (local only, not committed).

## How it is now

**Shape.** 35 units in 6 stages, each unit a situation with a can-do goal and an action title ("Order in a café or restaurant", "Talk about your week").

| Stage | Units | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 First contact | 1-6 | greet, be polite, introduce yourself, spell, numbers, personal details |
| 2 People and home | 7-12 | family, describing people and things, where you live, week, dates |
| 3 Time, food and money | 13-20 | time, daily routine, food, café, shopping, prices, services, hobbies |
| 4 Getting around | 21-26 | town, directions, transport, hotel, weather, appointments |
| 5 Health, work, everyday life | 27-32 | doctor, work, school, what you did yesterday, phone, invitations |
| 6 Exam readiness | 33-35 | exam skills, three mock papers (grammar and reading, listening, speaking) |

**Content.** All 821 words placed in exactly one unit (core words first). 305 phrases in 35 sections, the first five of each unit being its key phrases. 28 grammar chapters: 20 original, 6 new (und/oder/aber/denn, accusative pronouns, a little dative, war/hatte, werden, one more), 2 mock exams. Each chapter sits at the unit where it is first needed.

**Per unit.** Words, then phrases, then the grammar tip. A Guidebook sheet shows key phrases with audio, the tip, and "Revisit from earlier" practice for chapters taught in earlier units. A checkpoint (mixed recall of 20 weakest words, no effect on the review schedule) follows every third unit: 11 in all.

**Per lesson (vocab part of 4 words).** Meet (card, instant check, a sentence to hear) → Use it (pairs, gap-fills, a sentence to hear) → Say it (hard stretch ending with a whole German sentence typed from English, only from words already met). 15-26 steps, median 20. Phrase and grammar lessons carry the same phase labels; grammar goes multiple-choice first, then typed. In-lesson: combo bar goes gold at 3 and orange at 5, free labelled retry round, "can't listen" swaps in a text question (with Undo), wrong options prefer known words.

**Pace.** 12-week reference calendar; each learner's own start and target dates stretch it. Peak review load about 54 cards a day (old plan about 59). Everyone's German A1 was reset once; backup taken first.

## How it differs from Duolingo

| | Duolingo | LevelUp now |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | 8 sections, 1,030 units, A1 to B1+ | German A1 only, 35 units |
| A1 size | 70 units (Sections 1-3), about 4 lessons each | 35 units, parts of 4 words or 3 phrases |
| "A1" breadth | Goes to dative, Perfekt, imperative, adjective endings, two-way prepositions | Goethe A1 syllabus: stops short of most of that |
| Unit idea | One situation, can-do title | Same |
| Grammar | 1-3 tiny tips per unit, revisited many times | One chapter at its first-use unit, plus revisit cards |
| Lesson | About 11 mixed exercises, easy to hard, English to German at the end | Same arc, built from word parts; about 20 steps |
| Lesson mechanics | Combo bar, labelled mistakes round, listening skip swaps in text | Same three, built |
| Review | Practice inside the course | Spaced repetition plus a checkpoint every third unit |
| Exams | Not exam-focused | Mock papers in unit 35 |
| Extras we lack | Explain-my-answer, German Score, Stories, leagues | Not built (we have a Study helper chatbot, streaks, daily quest) |

## Known gaps

- Nothing beyond A1.
- Units are larger than Duolingo's (up to 30 words), so a unit takes longer to finish.
- Grammar is still a reading chapter plus questions, not woven into every lesson.
- Typed sentences use example sentences; we have no unit-level key-phrase capstone.
- Not played through to a pass after the lesson rebuild; the pass path (XP, word learning) is unchanged but was not re-run.
- Duolingo unit titles after Section 1 were not readable, so our unit list follows Goethe A1, not Duolingo's.
