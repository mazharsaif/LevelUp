# French A1 syllabus outline

Source: Simply French, "French A1 Syllabus" (https://www.simplyfrench.in/french-a1-syllabus), read 2026-11-21. Our own outline of the page's topic lists (paraphrased; open the link for the original wording). The page has no hours, fees or exam format; it links a separate DELF A1 exam guide (https://www.simplyfrench.in/delf-a1-exam).

A1 is the first CEFR stage: vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, listening, speaking, reading and writing, ending with simple everyday communication.

## Can-do goals (10)
Greet and introduce yourself · say where you live · describe your family · likes and dislikes · simple shopping · tell the time and date · accept and refuse politely · understand simple notices and posters · fill in basic forms · write simple postcards and messages.

## Grammar (23 points)
Personal pronouns · present tense · polite requests · asking questions · question words · "it is / this is / here is" (c'est, voici) · definite and indefinite articles · professions · numbers · demonstrative adjectives · gender and number · adjective agreement · affirmative and negative sentences · possession and possessive adjectives · expressions of quantity · countable and uncountable nouns (partitive) · weather expressions · time and date expressions · place and location expressions · "there is / there are" (il y a) · basic connectors.

## Sociocultural (7)
Greetings and introductions · polite expressions and etiquette · formal vs informal address (tu / vous) · expressing wants and polite requests · writing an informal letter · a postcard · a simple email.

## Vocabulary themes (21)
Alphabet · numbers · names · place of residence · personal belongings · classroom objects · nationalities · professions · marital status · countries and cities · hobbies · family · time and dates · prices and shopping · weather and climate · days of the week · colours · seasons · places · descriptive words.

## Speaking functions (23)
Greeting and saying goodbye · introducing yourself and others · exchanging personal information · spelling your name · age, nationality and profession · daily routine · contact details · family and hobbies · describing people and objects · ownership and quantity · prices · weather · asking for and giving information · time and dates · enquiring about services · places and locations · simple directions · basic instructions and advice · accepting or refusing politely · agreeing or disagreeing · simple conversations · asking for clarification.
